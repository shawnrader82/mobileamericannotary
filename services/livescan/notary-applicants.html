Keep your commission on track

Notary Live Scan Rules to Know.

These come straight from the California Secretary of State. Getting them right keeps your commission moving.

Exam First

Live Scan comes after you pass the Notary exam. If you have not passed yet, you do not need fingerprints yet.

Within One Year of Your Exam

Your fingerprints must be taken within one year of your exam date, or you have to take the exam again.

Use the Secretary of State Form

Print the Notary version of the Request for Live Scan Service form from the Secretary of State website. It sends your results to the right place.

Every Commission Term

You need a new Live Scan for each four-year commission term, including when you renew.

Keep Your Copy

Your copy of the form proves you paid the processing fee if your prints ever need to be retaken.

No Mailing Needed

You do not mail anything to the Secretary of State. The DOJ sends your results electronically.

Before we arrive

  • Your printed Notary Live Scan form from the Secretary of State website, with your personal details filled in.
  • A current photo ID, such as a driver license, state ID, or passport.
  • Clean, dry hands. Skip lotion the day of your appointment for the clearest prints.
  • A table and a nearby outlet where we can set up our equipment.

Common questions

Notary Applicant Live Scan FAQ.

How much does Live Scan cost for a Notary applicant?

With us, one applicant is $115 ($50 rolling fee plus $65 mobile fee) plus $49 in government fees: $32 for the California DOJ and $17 for the FBI. That is $164 total.

When do I get Live Scan for my Notary commission?

After you pass the Notary exam. The Secretary of State requires fingerprints within one year of your exam date, or you must take the exam again.

Which Live Scan form do Notary applicants use?

Use the Notary version of the Request for Live Scan Service form from the California Secretary of State website. Its agency codes send your results to the Secretary of State.

Do I need a new Live Scan when I renew my Notary commission?

Yes. The Secretary of State requires one set of fingerprints for each commission term, so renewing Notaries get a new Live Scan too.

Do I mail my Live Scan form to the Secretary of State?

No. The DOJ sends your results electronically. Keep your copy of the form until you receive your commission.

How can I check my Notary background check status?

Use the California DOJ applicant status website with the ATI tracking number on your Live Scan form and your date of birth. Name checks can take up to about four weeks.

Can you come to my home or office for Notary Live Scan?

Yes. We bring our Live Scan equipment to your home or office anywhere in Los Angeles, Orange, and Ventura Counties.
